About GramVani

Project Mission & Architecture

Empowering rural citizens through speech-driven grievance redressal and community skill discovery.

Project Objective

Rural citizens often face friction when reporting civic infrastructure issues (drinking water leakage, road potholes, transformer failure, drainage overflow) due to literacy barriers or inaccessible administrative portals. Furthermore, rural communities possess rich talent—educated youth and skilled tradesmen—yet lack a unified local directory to connect them.

GramVani unites these two essential pillars into a single responsive, mobile-first platform:

1. Voice Grievance Redressal

Enables villagers to speak in Telugu or English. Keyword-based heuristics automatically classify the civic department and determine urgency priority without needing confusing dropdowns.

2. Village Education & Skills

Enables educated youth to mentor village students and helps villagers hire local electricians, plumbers, carpenters, tailors, and mechanics while respecting user privacy.

Technology Stack

Semantic HTML5 Modern CSS3 (CSS Variables & Flex/Grid) Vanilla JavaScript (ES6 Modules) Browser Web Speech API Browser LocalStorage Chart.js Data Visualizations

Transparent Technical Disclaimers

  • LocalStorage Prototype: Data is saved within your current web browser and does not synchronize across separate physical devices.
  • Demonstration Scope: This application is an internship demonstration prototype for rural community empowerment. It is not connected to a live government portal.
  • Speech Recognition Constraints: Voice recognition depends on the browser's implementation of SpeechRecognition (best supported on Google Chrome, Edge, and Android web browsers). Telugu speech recognition relies on available device language packs. A complete manual text fallback is provided.
  • Heuristic Keyword Classification: Department categorization and priority assessment are implemented using transparent, rule-based keyword mapping rather than unverified AI claims.
  • Privacy by Default: Contact information in both directories is hidden by default to safeguard citizen privacy.
