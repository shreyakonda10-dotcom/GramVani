About GramVani

Project Mission & Architecture

Empowering rural citizens through voice-driven grievance redressal, volunteer advocacy, and transparent administration.

Project Objective

Rural citizens often face severe friction when reporting civic infrastructure issues (drinking water leakage, road potholes, transformer sparking, drainage overflow) due to literacy barriers, complicated administrative forms, or lack of direct access to panchayat officials.

GramVani transforms this experience into a voice-first, frictionless civic redressal platform combining:

1. Voice-First Redressal

Enables villagers to speak naturally in Telugu, Hindi, or English. Smart heuristics automatically identify civic departments and urgency without requiring any typing.

2. Village Volunteer Mode

Equips educated youth and ASHA workers to advocate for elderly neighbors, attaching photo evidence and logging complaints on their behalf.

3. Village Issues Map

Visualizes civic issues across Rampur's 6 localities, enabling community transparency and targeted administrative action.

4. 4-Stage Tracking & Ratings

Features a 4-stage tracking stepper with spoken audio updates, photo evidence display, and citizen satisfaction ratings.

Technology Stack

Semantic HTML5 Modern CSS3 (CSS Variables & Responsive Flex/Grid) Vanilla JavaScript (Zero External Paid API Dependencies) Browser Web Speech API (Recognition & Synthesis) HTML5 Canvas Client-Side Image Compression Browser LocalStorage Persistence Chart.js Data Visualizations

Transparent Technical Disclaimers

  • LocalStorage Prototype: Data is saved within your current web browser and does not synchronize across separate physical devices.
  • Demonstration Scope: This application is an internship demonstration prototype for rural community empowerment. It is not connected to a live government portal.
  • Speech Recognition Constraints: Voice recognition depends on the browser's implementation of SpeechRecognition (best supported on Google Chrome, Edge, and Android web browsers). Telugu and Hindi speech recognition rely on available device language packs. A complete manual text fallback is provided.
  • Heuristic Keyword Classification & Duplicate Detection: Department categorization and duplicate detection are implemented using transparent, rule-based keyword mapping rather than unverified AI claims.
  • Client-Side Photo Compression: Photos are downscaled using an in-memory HTML5 Canvas to 500px JPEG quality 0.7 to avoid LocalStorage quota overflow.
